Khan Mohd, Nguyen & Javaid (2022). A genuinely free, CC0-licensed survey
covering fusion across gesture, voice, sensor and haptic inputs, with the
uni-modal vs multi-modal taxonomy and the early/late fusion distinction laid
out clearly. Read it to place your own late-fusion decision in context and to
pick up the evaluation framing (task completion rate and error rate for
multimodal vs unimodal interfaces) rather than for any single architecture.
Full PDF verified freely available on arXiv.
